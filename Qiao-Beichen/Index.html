FTC IS COOL

The elements that make FTC a standout experience include:
* Real-World Engineering: Students aren't just snapping plastic bricks together; they are using professional-grade tools like 3D printing, CAD software, and metal manufacturing to build robots from scratch.
* Dynamic Games: Every year brings a completely new, thematic game. Teams must strategize how to maneuver their robots to pick up game pieces, score points in autonomous (code-only) periods, and outmaneuver opponents in alliance matches.
* College & Career Prep: Participants gain access to millions of dollars in college scholarships and learn career-ready skills like project management, teamwork, and fundraising.
* Gracious Professionalism: Unlike cutthroat competitions, FTC operates on a core value called "Gracious Professionalism". Teams are encouraged to compete fiercely but also help out their competitors—even fixing another team's robot so they can play.
